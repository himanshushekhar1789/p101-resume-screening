Job Title: Senior Backend Engineer
Department: Core Infrastructure Engineering
Location: Remote / Hybrid

About the Role:
We are looking for an experienced Senior Backend Engineer to design, scale, and maintain our high-throughput cloud microservices.

Responsibilities:
- Design and deploy scalable, fault-tolerant REST APIs and background services.
- Optimize relational database queries, schema designs, and indexing strategies.
- Collaborate with frontend and DevOps engineers to implement reliable end-to-end features.

Must-Have Requirements:
- Minimum 3+ years of professional backend development experience with Python.
- Minimum 2+ years of production experience developing web services using FastAPI.
- Verifiable hands-on experience with PostgreSQL relational database management.
- Minimum 4.0 years of general backend software engineering experience.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related technical discipline.

Nice-to-Have Requirements:
- Experience with Redis distributed caching and pub/sub.
- Hands-on experience containerizing services with Docker.
