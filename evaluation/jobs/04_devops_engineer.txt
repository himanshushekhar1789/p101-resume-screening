Job Title: Cloud DevOps & Infrastructure Engineer

- Minimum 2+ years of hands-on cloud engineering experience on AWS.
- Minimum 4.0 years of general DevOps or cloud infrastructure experience.

Nice-to-Have Requirements:
- Experience with Terraform Infrastructure as Code.
- Experience building automated CI/CD pipelines (e.g. GitHub Actions, Jenkins).
- AWS Certified Solutions Architect or Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA).
