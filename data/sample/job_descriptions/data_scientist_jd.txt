Job Title: Senior Data Scientist
Company: AI Horizon
Location: New York, NY (On-site)

- Master's degree in Artificial Intelligence, Computer Science, or Mathematics required

Preferred Qualifications:
- Ph.D. in Computer Science or related quantitative STEM field is a plus
- Hands-on experience deploying machine learning workflows on GCP or AWS is preferred
- Experience with Docker containerization and Kubernetes is desired
