Job Title: Senior Backend Software Engineer
Company: CloudNexus
Location: Remote (US / India)

About the Role:
CloudNexus is looking for an experienced Senior Backend Software Engineer to design, scale, and maintain our high-throughput cloud infrastructure and services.

Responsibilities:
- Design, build, and maintain scalable REST APIs and microservices using Python and FastAPI
- Architect and optimize relational database schemas and queries in PostgreSQL
- Build and maintain containerized microservices and automated CI/CD deployment pipelines
- Collaborate closely with frontend engineers, product managers, and site reliability engineers
- Participate in architectural reviews, sprint planning, and mentorship of junior engineers

Required Qualifications:
- 4+ years of professional backend software development experience
- 3+ years of hands-on experience with Python and FastAPI or Django
- Strong proficiency with PostgreSQL or MySQL
- Solid understanding of Git version control and collaborative development
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related technical field
- Must be authorized to work in the US or India

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with AWS or GCP is a plus
- Knowledge of Docker and Kubernetes is desired
- Master's degree in Computer Science is preferred
- AWS Certified Solutions Architect certification preferred
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
